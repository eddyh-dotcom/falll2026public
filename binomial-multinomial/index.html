How to approach these

1. Write the general term $\binom nk x^{n-k}y^k$ first, then solve for $k$ from the exponent you need.

2. A sum with $\binom nk c^k$ is $(1+c)^n$; a factor $k$ means differentiate; $\frac{1}{k+1}$ means integrate; a product of two binomials means Vandermonde.

3. For multinomials, check that the exponents add to $n$ before computing anything.

4. Test any identity at $n = 1, 2, 3$.
